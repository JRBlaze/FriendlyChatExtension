Friendly Chat New in 1.23.0
TwitchKickYouTube

YouTube joins your merged chat.

See YouTube live messages alongside Twitch and Kick, on the stream page you already watch.

01

Add a live chat

Paste a live-video or channel URL into YouTube, or choose Add YouTube chat on a live suggestion.

02

Save a channel link

In the stream panel's Settings → Cross-platform, link a YouTube channel to the Twitch or Kick channel you are watching. Its live chat can load automatically on future visits.

03

Choose where to send

Sending starts off. When YouTube's signed-in chat box is available, check the displayed account and select its send target for this visit. Use up to 200 characters; YouTube's normal chat restrictions still apply.

Allow YouTube access to get started

Access is optional. It lets Friendly Chat find live suggestions and load chat when you press Add or visit a channel with a saved YouTube link.

Sending needs your YouTube session to be available inside the attached chat. Being signed in on a normal YouTube tab may not be enough, especially in Firefox. Reading still works without sending. Captured messages have no reply or moderation controls.

A submitted message means YouTube cleared its editor, not that delivery was confirmed. An uncertain send is never retried automatically; check the chat before repeating it. No separate YouTube account connection is needed.

Channel lookups omit cookies and credentials. Attached chat loads YouTube content inside the stream page and may use cookies allowed by your browser. Captured messages are not saved to extension storage. You can revoke access in your browser's extension settings.

Checking YouTube access…

Set this up later from the Friendly Chat toolbar button or extension settings.
