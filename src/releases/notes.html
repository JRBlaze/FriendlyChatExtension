LATEST UPDATE

Version 1.23.4

  • More room for chat. Collapse Platforms to hide the Twitch, Kick and YouTube selectors and setup prompts. Found, unadded-channel notices stay visible in the compact header.
  • Compact send choices. Collapse Send to while keeping your active destinations visible, including the destination of a reply.
  • Your layout is remembered. Each section remembers whether you left it expanded or collapsed across visits and restarts. Both start expanded unless you choose to collapse them.
  • Know what changed. This page opens once after each version update. Reopen Release notes from the extension popup or Settings whenever you want.

Version 1.23.3

  • Reduced work for offscreen chat images and unnecessary layout and recent-emote redraws during busy chats.
  • Explicit YouTube send choices are remembered on this device for each Twitch or Kick host channel.
  • Twitch notification popups and their buttons stay clear of the overlay, including stacked notifications.

Version 1.23.2

  • The recent-emote bar shows up to eight newest Twitch and Kick emotes combined, fitting one row and reducing the count in narrower windows.

Version 1.23.1

  • Reuse recently sent Twitch and Kick emotes from a bar above the message box. Recent names are remembered on this device; the bar can be hidden in Settings.
  • YouTube sending selects automatically when its signed-in composer is ready. Check the displayed account and turn the destination off if preferred.
  • Click a YouTube chatter's name or use @autocomplete for a YouTube-only mention reply.
  • YouTube messages include supported emotes, badge images, links, mention highlights, paid messages and membership notices.
  • Show or hide YouTube in the feed, inspect an author's local recent messages, and open native YouTube chat for its other controls.
  • Right-click any Twitch, Kick or YouTube message to copy its body, including emote names, without its author header.

Version 1.23.0

  • Merge optional YouTube live chat into the overlay on Twitch and Kick. Add a video or channel URL, review a live suggestion, or save a channel link for future visits.
  • A one-time introduction helps enable optional YouTube access. Send through YouTube's native composer using your browser sign-in when available; Firefox offers sign-in access setup.
  • Press Up/Down in the message box to recall recently sent messages.
  • Fixed Firefox Backspace/Delete editing, Twitch Gigantify emote sizes, and visible, clickable Twitch/Kick notifications.
  • The overlay stays off Kick Drops, inventory and other non-chat dashboard pages; the live-chat dashboard at /stream remains supported.
