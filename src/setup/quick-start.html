Your streams. One conversation.

Welcome to Friendly Chat.

Read Twitch, Kick, and Youtube chats together on the stream page you already watch, and choose where each message goes.

01

Open a stream

Visit a Twitch or Kick channel. Friendly Chat appears over its chat. Refresh stream tabs that were open before installation.

02

Bring chats together

When the streamer is also live on the other platforms, add that chat to the same feed. You can also link channels yourself.

03

Choose where to send

Use Sent to to select Twitch, Kick, and/or Youtube. Enjoy emotes, favorites, replies and chat tools in one place.

Optional YouTube live chat is available from Settings. You choose which chats to add and where to send.

Use the accounts you chat with.

Connect your chats.

Connect Twitch and Kick, and allow access to YouTube chat. All three choices are optional.

Twitch

Your Twitch account

Sign in to Twitch and approve the connection in the window your browser opens.

Checking your connection…

Kick

Your Kick account

Sign in to Kick and approve the connection in the window your browser opens.

Checking your connection…

YouTube

Allow YouTube chat

Allow Friendly Chat to find and add YouTube live chat on Twitch and Kick pages. Sending uses your signed-in YouTube chat box.

Checking YouTube access…

Every choice here is optional. You can read Twitch and Kick without connecting, and connect later from the stream panel's Settings → Accounts. Connecting here does not change which account the Twitch or Kick website is signed in as. YouTube access can also be allowed later from the toolbar popup or All Settings.

Start with the defaults, or make it yours.

Ready to make it your own?

All Settings lets you adjust the overlay's appearance, emotes, chat behavior and optional YouTube access. The defaults are ready to use.

A few things you can customize

  • Text size, theme and overlay opacity
  • Which chats connect and how messages appear
  • Emote display and optional YouTube chat

You can always return to All Settings from the Friendly Chat toolbar button.

Setup complete

You're ready to chat.

Open a Twitch or Kick channel to get started. Refresh any stream tabs that were open before you installed Friendly Chat.

You can close this guide. Settings and account connections are available whenever you need them, and the quick-start guide stays in the toolbar popup and All Settings.
